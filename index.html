Hello, I’m Pratik.

I’m Pratik, a Staff Research Scientist at ServiceNow, where I lead research in AI Search and Agentic Search. I build retrieval, ranking, and evaluation systems, taking ideas from research to production. Previously, I was an early engineer at ThirdAI, which was acquired by ServiceNow. I’m an IIT Delhi computer science graduate, interested in making AI systems more useful, reliable, and efficient.

What I work on

My work sits at the intersection of information retrieval and practical machine learning: AI search systems, agentic retrieval, ranking, and the evaluation infrastructure needed to understand whether they actually work. I enjoy the full path from a research question to a dependable production system.

Earlier at ThirdAI, I helped build its enterprise search platform, distributed training systems, and CPU-efficient models. I was also a maintainer and core contributor to the ThirdAI ML Library, where I re-architected the Bolt C++ engine to support arbitrary computation graphs, including multi-output, recurrent, attention, and shared-weight tower architectures. I implemented tiled, SIMD-vectorized kernels and cache-friendly sharding that improved forward and backward throughput by 40% on commodity CPUs. I also built OpenMP-optimized feature pipelines and CI/CD with unit and integration testing, and exposed fine-grained activation and gradient hooks for custom metrics, debugging, and single-batch distributed training loops.

Related work: Distributed training with Ray · Bolt2.5B
